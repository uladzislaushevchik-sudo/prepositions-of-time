Предлоги времени

at · in · on + practice

AT — точное время
at 5 o’clock, at night, at breakfast, at 19, at Easter
ON — дни и даты
on Monday, on 5 May, on Christmas Day, on my birthday
IN — длинные периоды
in May, in 2026, in winter, in the morning, in 10 minutes
БЕЗ предлога
last Monday, next week, this month, every day, yesterday, today, tomorrow
Важно: on time = вовремя · in time = успеть
at the end = в конце · in the end = в итоге
since 2015 · by Friday · for 2 years · during the film
